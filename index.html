Tuckit

Drag, turn, fill the board.

Every level is a board and a handful of bright pieces that fit it exactly. Drag a piece onto the board, tap it to turn it, and tuck them all in until no cell is left.

Squares and rectangles first, then crosses, hearts, houses and boards with holes. No countdowns, no lives: take your time, and if you get stuck, a hint places the next piece for you.
